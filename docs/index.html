      The other NULL: what the data leaves out

      Method, data & limits

      How the walk is measured

      Streets, not circles. The distance is the shortest walk along OpenStreetMap: streets with or without sidewalks, paths, steps and squares. Motorways and ways closed to pedestrians are left out. A 500 m circle would promise more than the streets deliver.

      Entrances. Where an assembly point lies in a mapped park, square or school yard, you arrive as soon as you reach its edge. Otherwise you walk to the point itself.

      Buildings. Every OSM building takes the walk from its nearest street node. Residents come from WorldPop 2020 (100 m grid), shared out to the residential buildings in each cell by footprint area.

      500 m is the threshold of the original plan (Day 18 brief); it is roughly a 7-minute walk. You can read 250 m, 1 km and 2 km in the bar above.

      Data quality & limits

      The IBB dataset gives a point and a name, no area and no capacity. Areas here come from the OSM open space a point lies in and are an estimate. Population is a 2020 model, not a register. Roads blocked by debris, fences or crowds after an earthquake are not in any map. The map shows walking distance on a normal day, not safety or capacity.
